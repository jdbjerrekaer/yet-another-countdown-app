Privacy Policy

This app lets you create and manage countdown events. It works offline, and everything stays on your device.

Data Stored Locally

  • Countdown titles, dates, emojis, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Notification permissions are used only to schedule reminders for your countdowns.

Ads

  • The app displays ads to support development.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • If you consent, ads may be personalized. If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • On iOS, the system tracking permission prompt is used for consent.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your data to our servers.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email listed on the App Store listing.

Consent & Privacy Choices

You can update or revoke your ad consent at any time from the in-app privacy options, or contact the developer using the email listed on the App Store listing.
